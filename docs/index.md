# The One-Person LLC AI Playbook

*A friendly manual for running a small business with AI as your co-pilot — written like a friend would.*

## Who this is for

This manual is for exactly one kind of business: **yours, run by you.** Maybe you're a solo founder, an author, a reseller, or a side-hustle owner with a spreadsheet and too many browser tabs. If there's no payroll department, no marketing team, and no one to delegate to except... AI and future-you, this is for you.

Asdale Dynamics, LLC is itself a one-person Indiana LLC, so everything in here has been used by someone juggling the same hat-stack you are.

## The one rule of this playbook

> AI drafts. You decide.

Every page here assumes the same division of labor: AI is great at producing a first attempt, a summary, a suggestion, or a checklist. It's bad at knowing your customers, your money reality, or your gut. So it drafts; you approve.

## Companion project, not a repeat

This manual deliberately **does not teach bookkeeping**. Tracking income, expenses, and cash flow has its own home in the *cashflow-simple-ledger* project — a simpler, ledger-first approach to small-business money tracking.

The monthly money-date chapter here picks up where that ledger leaves off: it's about the **habit** of sitting down and the **decisions** you make afterward, not about where to type the numbers. Use both projects together: the ledger is the tool, this playbook is the ritual.

## How to use this manual

1. Read [Honest limits](honest-limits.html) first. Seriously — it's short, and it keeps you safe with the other pages.
2. Pick ONE routine to start (the money-date is the best first pick).
3. Use the [worksheet](worksheets/index.html) version until the routine feels automatic.
4. Add the next routine a month later. Nothing here needs to be adopted all at once.

Everything is local-first: markdown files you can read offline, a build script that turns them into a small HTML site, and no accounts, keys, or cloud anything required.
